PROPERTY OF A TEN-YEAR-OLD

FIELD NOTES

WASD moves. Mouse looks. Click the world to capture the mouse; Escape releases it. If your browser blocks mouse capture, drag to look or use the arrow keys. Shift sprints. F toggles your torch. E interacts. J opens this journal.

Battle animals using their moves. Canines have an advantage over felines, felines over rodents, rodents over canines. Speed decides who acts first. Weaken wild animals before throwing a carrier. Trainer animals cannot be captured.

Use medkits or switch to another animal when needed. Captured animals join your party (maximum six); further captures go to Gary’s clinic storage. Transfer stored animals through Your Animals while at home or near Gary. The species register keeps your discoveries even when animals are in storage. Return home or visit Gary to restore your party for free. If your entire party faints, you wake up at home.

Defeat the eight district leaders in order. Each badge permits travel between previously visited league offices using the map. Then return to Gary for the four-round county championship.

This permanently clears this browser’s campaign.
